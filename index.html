The core constraint: the data lived across multiple third-party fund and strategy APIs with inconsistent schemas, making simple automation brittle. The business needed something that could reason across sources, not just retrieve from one.

The Architecture Decision

The first instinct is always to reach for a RAG pipeline. But this problem required cross-source reasoning, not retrieval — the system needed to synthesize signals from fund performance data, strategy commentary, and historical reference points, then generate a coherent narrative. A single-step LLM call would hallucinate. A naive RAG would retrieve without reasoning.
